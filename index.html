Publications

  • Anchored Fusion for Automated Airway Segmentation in Pediatric and Adult CT 2026
    arXiv preprint, submitted to ISBI 2027 · arXiv · code · project

    Hüseyin Umut Işık, Ege Mert Balcık, Duygu Atasoy, Asutosh Sahu, Şeyda Ertekin, Sıla Kurugöl.

    A-Fuse takes the union of three airway models and keeps the connected component anchored to one of them, so the airway tree survives where largest-component selection picks the wrong object. 94.15% Dice on pediatric CT with no pediatric training data.

  • ARC-CT: Anatomy-Routed Contrastive Vision-Language Learning for 3D Chest CT 2026
    MICCAI 2026, TIA Workshop · arXiv · code · project

    Hüseyin Umut Işık, Mehmet Alp Özaydın, Sıla Kurugöl, Şeyda Ertekin.

    A region-aware vision-language model for 3D chest CT. Organ-masked queries and a soft contrastive loss align scans with radiology reports, giving zero-shot classification of 18 abnormalities without manual labels.

  • LuMon: A Comprehensive Benchmark and Development Suite with Novel Datasets for Lunar Monocular Depth Estimation 2026
    CVPR 2026, AI4SPACE Workshop · arXiv

    Aytaç Sekmen, Fatih Emre Güneş, Furkan Horoz, Hüseyin Umut Işık, Mehmet Alp Özaydın, Onur Altay Topaloğlu, Şahin Umutcan Üstündaş, Yurdasen Alp Yeni, Halil Ersin Söken, Erol Şahin, Ramazan Gökberk Cinbiş, Sinan Kalkan.

    A benchmark of monocular depth estimation models on lunar simulations and real lunar data.
