About

I am currently a research intern at the Computational Radiology Laboratory at Boston Children's Hospital / Harvard Medical School, where I work on medical image analysis. My research interests are in computer vision and deep learning, in particular monocular depth estimation for lunar robotics and medical imaging.

On the side, quantum computing is a hobby I take seriously: my teams have won first place at the EPFL Quantum Hackathon (Quandela Challenge) and IQM QuantumHACK Madrid, and received an honorable mention at MIT iQuHACK.
